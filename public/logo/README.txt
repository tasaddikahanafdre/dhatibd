=======================================================
             DHATI STORE BRAND ASSETS FOLDER
=======================================================

1. STORE LOGO (Header Brand Logo):
-------------------------------------------------------
Paste your store logo here with the filename:
logo.png

Requirements:
- Filename: logo.png (lowercase or uppercase)
- Format: PNG (transparent background recommended) or JPG/SVG/WEBP
- Suggested size: Height between 60px and 120px (e.g. 300x80px or 400x100px)

Behavior:
- Once you paste logo.png here, it will automatically display in the header in place of the text "DHATI"!
- If you remove logo.png, it will automatically switch back to the "DHATI" text.


2. STORE FAVICON (Browser Tab Icon):
-------------------------------------------------------
Paste your site's favicon icon here with the filename:
fav.png

Requirements:
- Filename: fav.png (or fav.PNG, fav.ico, fav.jpg, fav.svg, fav.webp)
- Format: PNG (square 1:1 ratio recommended, e.g. 32x32, 64x64, 128x128, 512x512)
- Transparent background recommended

Behavior:
- Once you paste fav.png into this folder:
  D:\Dhati Website\public\logo\fav.png
- It will immediately and automatically become the live browser tab favicon for this website!
- If you remove fav.png, it will automatically switch back to the default Dhati favicon icon.
